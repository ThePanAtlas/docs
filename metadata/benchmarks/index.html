    Benchmarks¶

    A benchmark in panAtlas is described once, by a registry entry, an ontology classification, a taxonomy placement, a suite-fit verdict and a mapping from its items to concepts. Results attach to it separately (see Evaluation records). This page documents every field of that description. Counts were computed with python3 from the files at repo HEAD 57505c6 on 2026-11-10.

    Decontamination block (decontam)¶

    Field Meaning
    exact_doc_uid.checked Whether the exact-document membership check has been run (16 true, 90 false)
    exact_doc_uid.items Items checked
    exact_doc_uid.matched_either Items found in either training ledger
    exact_doc_uid.verdict Plain statement, for example "no verbatim-document match (held out at the exact-document level)"
    method "exact doc_uid membership (blake2b digest_size=16, raw text) vs doc_ledger_v4/v5 crosswalk; holdout_status from KOS decontamination-holdouts audit"

    On the graph node the same object also carries holdout_status and holdout_note. The status values on the 106 llm-benchmark nodes are unchecked 85, not_held_out 10, provably_held_out 5, partial 4, proven_trained_on 2.

    Decontamination fields differ from the catalog document

    docs/evaluation-dimensions-and-metadata.md (section G) documents exact_doc_uid = {checked, matched_either, matched_v4, matched_v5}. The data has {checked, items, matched_either, verdict}; no record has matched_v4 or matched_v5.

    The audit block is smaller than documented

    docs/evaluation-dimensions-and-metadata.md (section D) lists 23 audit fields, including purpose, used_for, how_to_run, engine, kos_result, reporting, quality_notes, rationale and caveats. None of these is in the served registry at HEAD. purpose, used_for, how_to_run, engine, kos_result and caveats exist in web/benchmark_audit.json (106 records), but build_eval_map.py does not copy them; quality_notes and rationale exist only in the benchmark_suite_fit.json dossiers.

    The benchmark ontology (facets)¶

    docs/benchmark-ontology.md defines 26 dimensions in seven groups (A to G) and states that no model is run to produce it: it is characterization only. web/benchmark_ontology_facets.json holds one row per benchmark for 106 benchmarks. Each row has 27 keys: benchmark (the name), 22 controlled facets, and four free-text fields (subdomain, the_document, primary_metric, notes).

    Three of the documented dimensions are not in the facets file:

    executability audit.executable in the registry (same value set)
    suite_decision audit.suite in the registry (same value set)
    engine engine in web/benchmark_audit.json only, as free text (n/a for 38; otherwise strings such as "lm-eval-harness (pristine); stock task present"), not the documented controlled values; not copied into the registry

    The per-benchmark table in the ontology document mislabels a column

    In docs/benchmark-ontology.md, the per-benchmark table's column headed "scoring" contains scoring_determinism values (deterministic-programmatic, reference-metric, …), not scoring_method values.

    The decision axis is group G. concept_fit grades whether individual items relate to concepts: strong (each item maps to one dominant concept), partial (diffuse, or the scored quantity is a relation or inference), weak (only coarse mapping), none (no item-to-concept relation). panatlas_class gives the operational class: A-programmatic (deterministic or execution scoring, suite-eligible), B-llm-judge (the score depends on a judge), mcq-standard, and probe-diagnostic (no item-to-concept relation).

    Suite fit: benchmark_suite_fit.json¶

    The suite-fit audit decides which benchmarks belong to the concept-relational suite. Its rule, stated in suite.principles, has a decisive first test: a benchmark belongs only if each individual item is a document that maps to a dominant concept grounded in MeSH (clinical and biomedical) or Wikipedia (general). This is judged before and independently of score quality. Reporting prevalence is the second criterion.

    Key Content
    dossiers (106) Per benchmark: benchmark, capability, what_it_does, how_it_works, the_document, concept_fit, concept_fit_how, reporting_prevalence, quality_notes, recommendation, rationale
    suite.suite_core (17) benchmark, arm, concept_fit, reporting_prevalence, the_document, concept, why_core, open_work
    suite.suite_secondary (15) Same, with why_secondary
    suite.diagnostic_or_excluded (73) benchmark, concept_fit, category, reason
    suite.add_to_fill_gaps (24) name, concept_fit, reporting_prevalence, availability, gap_filled, priority
    suite.coverage_map, suite.medical_coverage, suite.principles Free-text analysis
    missing.candidates (29) Benchmarks not in the registry: name, what, how, the_document, concept_fit, reporting_prevalence, fills_gap, availability

    The core suite is MedQA (USMLE 4-opt), MedMCQA, MedXpertQA (Text), Medical-MMLU (9-subject), MMLU, MMLU-Pro, ARC-Challenge, ARC-Easy, OpenBookQA, PubMedQA, MIRAGE, SciFact, AfriMed-QA, TruthfulQA, SimpleQA, GSM8K and MATH. The secondary suite is Medbullets, MedExpQA, CareQA, MedNLI, MEDEC, Med-HALT, CommonsenseQA, HellaSwag, PIQA, WinoGrande, BoolQ, MedRAG, ASDiv (CoT), MathQA and GPQA.

    Three suite-decision tallies disagree

    The core (17) and secondary (15) lists agree everywhere. The rest does not: dossiers[].recommendation gives candidate 43, exclude 31; suite.diagnostic_or_excluded lists 73 benchmarks; the registry's audit.suite gives exclude 61, candidate 9 (over 102 audited entries). Treat audit.suite as the value the site reads.

    Question → concept mapping¶

    The mapping gives each benchmark item a concept, so a model's per-item correctness rolls up to per-concept accuracy. It exists in two generations. Item text and answers are not in the repository; only distributions and item-to-concept rows are.

    First mapping (qconcept)¶

    Designed in web/BENCHMARK-QUESTION-MAPPING.md and implemented in build_question_concept_map.py. Each question is embedded with BioClinical-ModernBERT-large (1024-d, masked mean pool, max_length 1024, L2-normalised), assigned to the nearest of 4,096 corpus cell centroids, and given that cell's dominant concept, the same path the corpus documents use.

    Row field (qconcept_<bench>.jsonl) Meaning
    question_id Source id, else <bench>__<index> (six-digit, zero-padded)
    doc_uid blake2b hex digest of the question text
    benchmark bench-<slug>
    concept Concept node id, or unmapped
    cell Corpus cell key
    cosine Cosine to the cell centroid

    The repository holds the 21 distribution files ({benchmark, n, distribution}) and qconcept_stats.json (per benchmark: n, n_concepts, top_concept, top_share, entropy_bits, mean_cosine, poor_fit_frac). The row files are on the cluster. The 21 benchmarks are AfriMed-QA, AGIEval LSAT-AR, ARC-Challenge, ARC-Easy, ASDiv, BoolQ, CommonsenseQA, COPA, GPQA, GSM8K, HellaSwag, LAMBADA, Medical-MMLU, MedMCQA, MedQA, MedXpertQA, MMLU-Pro, MMLU, OpenBookQA, PubMedQA and WinoGrande. This mapping feeds concept_accuracy.json and the measured_on edges (Evaluation records).

    The doc_uid in question rows is not the ledger doc_uid

    build_question_concept_map.py computes hashlib.blake2b(text).hexdigest() with the default 64-byte digest. The training-ledger identity used for decontamination is blake2b(raw_text, digest_size=16). The two cannot be joined.

    Judge-gated mapping (qconcept2)¶

    build_concept_remap.py wires a corrected mapping from web/pipeline/qconcept2/. Its rows carry question_id, benchmark, concept and cosine. Over the 20 files there are 48,931 rows, of which 2,925 are unmapped. The method string the script stamps is "judge-gated: BGE-large retrieval + gemma-4-31B concept judge". The script:

    1. creates the Wikipedia-grounded general concepts from general_concept_space.json (homes for math, science and reading items);
    2. rebuilds each benchmark's distribution, stored on the node as topics = {n, unmapped, distribution};
    3. wires the 15 largest concepts per benchmark as covers_topic edges with n = number of items (300 edges at HEAD).

    hygiene_enrich.py then derives a grounded_in edge (attribute via: mesh:<UI>) from a benchmark to every MeSH-grounded concept it reaches through covers_topic, tests or covers (239 edges).

    Design, graph and served registry disagree on the mapping

    BENCHMARK-QUESTION-MAPPING.md requires one embedding space (BioClinical-ModernBERT) and lists MedMCQA (270 questions) as the only benchmark mapped. The served graph's topic edges come from the judge-gated BGE mapping over 20 benchmarks (MedMCQA is not among them). The served benchmarks_llm.json still carries topics on 6 entries and topics_status "pending general concept space" on 15, while the graph nodes carry judge-gated topics on 20. The per-concept accuracies on concept nodes still use the first mapping's concept ids.

    Benchmark nodes in the graph¶

    llm-benchmark (106)¶

    Built from the registry. Fields on every node: id, label, kind, layer (concept), arm, metric, gated, measures, composition, kos_usage, benchmark (always true), modality (always text), testing_type, domain, concept_class (always evaluation), decontam. On some: artifact_types, artifact_type_labels, artifact_type_via (96); tier (29); topics, topics_status (20); grounding, wp_topic, wp_url (7: intrinsic methods given a Wikipedia grounding by hygiene_enrich.py).

    artifact_type_via records how the artifact types were assigned: override (34, from benchmark_overrides in artifact_types.json) or facet:<task_format> (from benchmark_task_format_map). Ten nodes have none; representation diagnostics are deliberately untyped.

    Edges out of an llm-benchmark: is_type, in_domain, tests, covers_topic, grounded_in, has_artifact_type. Edges into it: scored and evaluated_on from models.
